# Media Mind features list (PRD v2.1)

Date: 15 Nov 2026. Agreed with Sadril. Every item maps to a task on the build tracker (https://claude.ai/artifact/42JgJXzgW7g97xrQ2n6Med).

## 1. Home
- Spend to date, latest month, comparison with last FY, media mix
- Monthly spend by media, spend by business
- Snapshots: TV, Digital, OOH
- Brand spend table

## 2. Campaign Performance
- TV (ATL): 13 categories at a glance (GRP, spend, SOV, SOE, rank, leader). One category in detail with charts.
- Digital: Meta, YouTube, TikTok ads, exactly as uploaded, in USD (confirmed 15 Nov 2026). This is the only page in USD.
- OOH: map, division table, cost by month, site list.
- Influencer: boosted posts by platform and brand.

## 3. Media Spend
- Monthly Activity Calendar (Gantt chart and the Excel-style sheet)
- FY Media Spend (brand by month, business totals, last FY)

## 4. Channel Wise Spend
- Electronic (by TV channel), Digital (by platform), Press & Radio (by name), month by month

## 5. Billing
- Overview, vendor statement
- Full 22-column billing sheet, with a column picker (choose which columns to show)
- OOH bill status

## 6. Billboards (OOH)
- Bangladesh map with 8 divisions and pins
- Hover shows photo, brand, size, vendor, cost. Click opens the site.
- Click a division to zoom and see its table
- Add sites four ways: master sheet, Add billboard, paste rows, set on map
- Photos per site
- Cost spread by day

## 7. Data in
- Upload his usual Excel file. No template.
- Auto-detect the file type
- Preview with warnings, then confirm
- Upload History with restore. Original file kept.
- Manual entry for channel cost, FY spend and OOH bills

## 8. Data out
- Download on every report: Source Excel, Excel, CSV, PDF, PowerPoint
- Downloads follow the filters on screen

## 9. Filters and rules
- Business, brand and month filters
- FY July to June, with the year in month labels. Months come from the uploaded data, so any months work (nothing hard-coded to FY 2026-27).
- Real numbers only, no projections
- Full official brand names
- BDT Mn everywhere, USD only on Digital Performance
- Admin-set USD rate for other digital figures

## 10. Access and settings
- Everyone with the link reads. No sign-in.
- Admin signs in and edits. Lock after wrong tries.
- Backup (Download backup)
- Mobile and laptop layout, light and dark mode

## Later (not in first release)
- Per-person access (staff ID, sections, brands)
- Live Meta, Google and TikTok data
- Influencer creator fees
- Photos for all 40 sites (data task)
